Getting started

Create an account as an owner if you're looking for a Sittr, or as a Sittr if you'd like to list your services. Browsing Sittrs and reading reviews doesn't require an account.

Sending a request

Once you find a Sittr you like, open their profile and send a hire request with your dates and notes. You can track its status from "My requests" once logged in.

Managing your listing

Sittrs can edit their own listing, pricing, and services at any time from "My profile" in the header.
