2. What we collect

We collect only what's needed to run the service:

  • Account data: username, email address, and a securely hashed password (we never store passwords in plain text).
  • Sittr listing data (if you register as a Sittr): name, contact email, phone number, city, bio, hourly rate, services, pet types, and an optional profile photo.
