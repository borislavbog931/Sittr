About Sittr

Designed to make pet care choices feel simple and trustworthy.

Sittr helps you find reliable, caring people for your best friend quickly, clearly, and without unnecessary friction.

Goal

Help owners choose with confidence.

Trust

Clarity through profiles, services, and reviews.

Speed

Request a caretaker in minutes.

Our purpose

Pets are family. Sittr makes it easy to compare caretakers by what matters most: services, pet types, location, price, and reviews.

Mission

Make finding a caretaker feel simple while giving owners enough transparency to trust their choice.

Motivation

Sittr was founded in 2023 to reduce the uncertainty pet owners feel when they can find someone online but cannot tell who is genuinely right for their pet.

What you can do

Browse and filter

Search by city, service, pet type, and price.

Read reviews

Ratings are still 1 to 10, shown with a more polished presentation.

Send a request

Reach out to a caretaker without needing an account first.

Best friends first

Care you can trust should be easy to recognize.

That principle drives the browsing experience, the profile detail pages, and the way requests and reviews work throughout Sittr.
