What are cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of data stored in your browser that let a site remember information between visits.

What Sittr uses today

Sittr currently sets a single essential cookie: a session cookie that keeps you logged in and remembers your chosen language (English/Bulgarian). Without it, login and the language switch wouldn't work, so it can't be turned off within the site.

What Sittr does not use

We do not currently use advertising, analytics, or tracking cookies of any kind.

Managing cookies in your browser

You can block or delete cookies through your browser's settings, but doing so will prevent you from staying logged in to Sittr.

If this changes

If we introduce analytics or advertising cookies in the future, we'll update this policy and add a proper consent choice before any non-essential cookie is set.
